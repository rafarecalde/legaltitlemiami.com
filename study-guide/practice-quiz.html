Practice quiz

25 questions with instant grading and explanations.

25 questions drawn from the study guide chapters. Answer them all, then select Grade my quiz to see your score and an explanation with the legal source for each answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. How many questions are on the Florida Sales Associate state exam?

Answer: 100. The DBPR Candidate Information Booklet lists 100 multiple-choice questions, 3.5 hours, passing score 75.

2. What is the minimum passing score on the state exam?

Answer: 75. A score of 75 or higher is required (DBPR CIB).

3. Unless otherwise established in writing, a Florida licensee is presumed to be a:

Answer: Transaction broker. § 475.278(1)(b).

4. Which duty is owed by a single agent but NOT a transaction broker?

Answer: Loyalty. Loyalty is one of the nine single-agent duties; transaction brokers aren't fiduciaries (§ 475.278).

5. Under Rule 61J2-14.008, a broker must deposit escrow funds 'immediately,' meaning no later than:

Answer: The end of the third business day following receipt. Rule 61J2-14.008(3).

6. Which is NOT one of the escape procedures for conflicting escrow demands?

Answer: Disbursing to the party the broker believes is right. § 475.25(1)(d)1. lists EDO, arbitration, litigation and mediation.

7. If mediation is used for an escrow dispute, it must be completed within:

Answer: 90 days of the last demand. § 475.25(1)(d)1.

8. The maximum administrative fine the Commission may impose per count is:

Answer: $5,000. § 475.25(1).

9. Post-licensing education for a sales associate may not exceed how many classroom hours?

Answer: 45. § 475.17(3)(a).

10. Continuing education requires at least how many classroom hours each biennium?

Answer: 14. § 475.182.

11. A Florida deed conveying land must be signed in the presence of:

Answer: Two subscribing witnesses. § 689.01.

12. A conveyance to two unmarried people that is silent about survivorship creates a:

Answer: Tenancy in common. § 689.15.

13. How many acres are in a section?

Answer: 640. A section is one square mile, or 640 acres.

14. How many acres are in the NE ¼ of the SW ¼ of a section?

Answer: 40. 640 ÷ 4 ÷ 4 = 40.

15. Florida mortgages are treated as:

Answer: A specific lien, not a conveyance of title. § 697.02.

16. Mortgage foreclosure in Florida is:

Answer: Judicial, in equity. § 702.01.

17. Outside Miami-Dade, deed doc stamps are:

Answer: 70¢ per $100. § 201.02(1)(a).

18. Doc stamps on a $250,000 Broward deed are:

Answer: $1,750. 2,500 units × $0.70 = $1,750.

19. The nonrecurring intangible tax on a $200,000 mortgage is:

Answer: $400. $200,000 × 0.002 = $400 (§ 199.133).

20. Florida real property is assessed as of:

Answer: January 1. § 192.042(1).

21. Property taxes paid in November receive a discount of:

Answer: 4%. § 197.162(1).

22. A buyer of a condo unit is liable for the prior owner's unpaid assessments that came due up to the transfer:

Answer: Jointly and severally with the prior owner. § 718.116(1)(a).

23. A condo association must issue an estoppel certificate within how many business days of a request?

Answer: 10. § 718.116(8).

24. A contract for the sale of land must be in writing under:

Answer: The statute of frauds, § 725.01. § 725.01.

25. Under the Marketable Record Title Act, the root of title period is generally:

Answer: 30 years. § 712.02.

Practice questions written for this site. They are not actual exam questions and do not reflect the exam's difficulty or weighting.
