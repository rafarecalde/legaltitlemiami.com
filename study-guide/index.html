Florida real estate exam study guide

Free chapters and a practice quiz for the Florida Sales Associate exam. No sign-up required.

Free and open to everyone. No sign-up, no email required. This guide is general exam review. It is not a substitute for the state-required pre-licensing course, is not approved for continuing-education credit, and is not affiliated with DBPR, the Florida Real Estate Commission or Pearson VUE.

Built around the official exam outline

The Florida Sales Associate exam has 100 multiple-choice questions, a 3.5-hour time limit and a passing score of 75, per the DBPR Candidate Information Booklet (effective January 2025). The chapters below map to the booklet's 19 content areas, cite the Florida Statutes directly, and include worked math.
