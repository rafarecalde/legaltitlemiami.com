Before you sign

Bring us in before the contract is signed

Title and closing work usually starts once a contract is signed. If you involve Recalde & Friedman, PLLC earlier, we can review the terms while they can still be changed.

  • Contract and addenda review. We read the purchase contract, riders and addenda and point out terms that affect title, the escrow deposit or the closing.
  • Dates that line up. We check the closing, escrow deposit, financing, inspection and association-approval deadlines against each other and the calendar.
  • Early title and lien checks. A preliminary look at recorded mortgages, liens, judgments, open permits and who has authority to sign.
  • HOA and condo items. Association approval requirements, estoppels and, for condos, the required disclosures, including milestone inspection and reserve study information.
  • Issues raised while terms are negotiable. A problem found before signing can be handled in the contract itself, instead of through extensions or amendments later.
  • A smoother path to closing. The file is already open, so the title commitment, estoppels and payoffs can be ordered as soon as the contract is signed.

Before we review a contract, we confirm in writing who we represent in the transaction.

Process

How a closing works with us

  1. Send us the contract. Share the signed contract and contact details for the buyer, seller, agents and lender.
  2. Title search & commitment. We search the public records and issue a title commitment listing requirements and exceptions.
  3. Clear the conditions. Payoffs, estoppel letters, open permits, liens and entity documents are requested and reviewed.
  4. Settlement figures. We coordinate the closing disclosure or settlement statement with the lender and the parties.
  5. Sign & fund. Signing in Brickell, Aventura or by arrangement. Funds are verified before disbursement.
  6. Record & issue policy. The deed and mortgage are recorded, and the title policies are issued.
