How a closing works with us

  1. Open the order. Send the signed contract and contact details for the buyer, seller, agents and lender.
  2. Title search & commitment. We search the public records and issue a title commitment listing requirements and exceptions.
  3. Clear the conditions. Payoffs, estoppel letters, open permits, liens and entity documents are requested and reviewed.
  4. Settlement figures. We coordinate the closing disclosure or settlement statement with the lender and the parties.
  5. Sign & fund. Signing in Brickell, Aventura or by arrangement. Funds are verified before disbursement.
  6. Record & issue policy. The deed and mortgage are recorded, and the title policies are issued.
